## The marvel as a line item

- Id: grzedowicz.magic.1        Binding: hard
- Effect: A fantastic power reads as real because it arrives as a cost, a permit or a delay in the ordinary order, not as a display.
- Mechanism: (1) The power appears in the city's paperwork, prices or habits. (2) Its limit is shown through what someone could not do or had to pay. (3) The consequence falls on a bystander's work. (4) The effect itself is described only as far as a clerk would note it.
- Test: Is the power met first as an entry on a list, a price or a refusal, with no spectacle?
- In this world: "Fantasy in the background": a few things in the city are not natural and an office handles them. No magic system is fixed by this card; that is for the pitch round and a decision.
- Plain: A hedge-mage lifted the barrels onto the barge with a spell, and the porters watched in awe.
- Applied: Barrels above the third tier go by the lifter's mark, not by hand: the lifter's fee, and a day's wait until the lifter is free. Janek's crew carried four tiers that morning, and the lifter's mark stayed unused on the board.
- Avoid: Showy effects. Magic that solves the plot. A rule-set invented on the card.
- Evidence: Pan Lodowego Ogrodu (Fabryka Słów; edition not given), no locator; not read. ## The office known by its marks

- Id: mieville.institution.1        Binding: soft
- Effect: A powerful body feels real, and a little frightening, when it is met through its forms, marks and delays and seldom in person.
- Mechanism: (1) Show the body's trace: a stamp, a coloured thread, a form number. (2) People avoid asking where it sits. (3) When an official appears, they speak briefly and in procedure. (4) Its reach is shown by what is quietly not done.
- Test: Is the body shown by its traces and not by a description of its building or its leaders?
- In this world: The office that reconciles old rules and new, and handles whatever in the city is not natural. Its name and powers are for the pitch round.
- Plain: The Office of Old Measures was a powerful organisation in a large building by the river.
- Applied: The sack came back with a green thread through the neck. Nobody at the pitch asked who had put it there, and nobody unpicked it. At the weigh-house the clerk looked at the thread, and the fee was not asked.
- Avoid: A named secret police. Copying a source's enforcing body, its name or its function. Making the office omnipotent.
