The clerk at the upper weigh-house stamped her slip at ten past one. The stamp was for the delivery and not for the hour, so the fee came to her in full, and she stood with the coins in her hand and nothing to say about it.

Behind her the old porter had his boots off again.

"Short?" he asked.

"Full."

"Then they've changed it," he said, "or they've forgotten it."

She put the coins in her boot. The next load was a barrel for the cooper's, third tier, with the lifter's mark already chalked on the lid. She did not ask which of the two he meant.
