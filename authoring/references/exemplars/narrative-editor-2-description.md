purpose: Shows an edit of descriptive prose: mood words and a stock comparison replaced by a checkable comparison and a cost. Illustrative and not canon.

## Draft

It was a dark, oppressive morning on the river, and a feeling of dread hung over the quay as Marek hauled the heavy crate, which was huge, like a mountain.

## Tests applied

- One material detail carries the mood (tone): fails. "Dark", "oppressive" and "dread" are mood words, and no detail replaces them.
- Comparison from the trade (prose): fails. "Like a mountain" would fit any city's story.
- Checkable: nothing in the draft can be checked. "Heavy" and "huge" give no measure.

## Edit

On the quay the barges lay two deep with their ropes crossed. Marek took the crate by both handles. It was twice the weight of a full bucket of brine, and the wet rope on the stone had already got into his sleeves.

## Notes for the writer

Kept: the morning, the quay, the crate and Marek's effort. Dropped: the dread. If the scene needs more dread, add a consequence (a missed bell, a refused slip) and not an adjective. The weight is now a measure a reader can check against the cargo. Not changed: the identity of the crate.
