## Old Stock, New Seal

**Offer (the cooper).** "Barrels for the upper weigh-house. The seals are the new pattern. The barrels are old stock."

**Gate (the clerk).** "New seal on old stock is a transfer. A transfer needs the cooper's signature. No signature, no crossing."

**Choice.**

- Go back for the signature. Result: the delivery is late, and the cooper says the clerk was right.
- Split the load and send the old-seal barrels first. Result: the clerk passes them and asks nothing about the rest.
- Argue the pattern. Result: the clerk writes your name in the margin and passes nothing.

**Close.** "Delivered. The ledger will show the transfer."

**Archive hook (found later, not told).** A ledger entry shows the transfer rule was written after a fire. It does not say why the cooper signs.

## Design notes

- Consequence is practical: lateness, a margin note, a split load. Nothing is a reward value.
- The player learns the rule by meeting it at the gate. The reason is in the archive and costs a visit.
