## Sign at the weigh-house

Slips are stamped by delivery, not by the hour. A late load is a late load. Complaints go on the board, east side. Ask for the clerk on duty.

## Codex: The lifter's mark

Barrels above the third tier go by the lifter. The mark is chalked, never painted. The fee is the lifter's. The wait is yours.

## Page from the porters' book

Touch the post before the first load. Do not cross the Salt Stair at noon. Do not ask the post.
