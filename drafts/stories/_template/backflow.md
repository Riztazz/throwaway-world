# Back-flow

Consequential facts this story established that the bible does not yet hold (a motive, a rule, a date, a relationship). The canon custodian fills this after a draft; nothing here is canon. The user reviews the rows in a batch (rules/STORIES.md).

Status: `proposed`, `filed` (a change record or draft page exists, named in Proposal), `declined` or `story-local`.
